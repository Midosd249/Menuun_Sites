# Editorial gallery

## Expected filenames
- `atelier-interior.webp` — wide view of the interior.
- `barber-at-work.webp` — craft/action portrait.
- `tools-and-textures.webp` — premium tools and material details.
- `signature-finish.webp` — finished haircut detail.

Recommended export: 1600 px on the long edge, WebP, sRGB. Use a mix of wide and portrait source compositions so the grid feels curated rather than repetitive.

## Nano Banana Pro prompt template
Create a photorealistic high-end editorial photograph for a contemporary men's grooming atelier in Riyadh, image category: [CATEGORY]. Visual subject: [SPECIFIC SUBJECT]. Deep charcoal and dark walnut palette, warm directional lighting, tactile materials, realistic skin/hair/tool details, subtle film grain, understated luxury, contemporary fashion-campaign composition, rich shadows without losing detail. No text, no logo, no watermark, no collage, no split frame, no artificial HDR.

Categories:
- Interior: architecturally beautiful grooming studio, dark timber, stone, brushed metal, elegant mirrors, clean stations.
- Craft: barber's hands and comb working through a client's hair, candid documentary angle.
- Tools: carefully arranged professional clippers, combs, brush, towel and metal tools on a dark work surface.
- Finish: close-up of a clean modern men's haircut and natural hair texture.

Each gallery image must have a distinct composition and subject. Avoid fake signage and illegible text.
