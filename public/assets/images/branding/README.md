# Branding assets — ATELIER 09

**Expected file:** `atelier-09-logo.png`  
**Path:** `public/assets/images/branding/atelier-09-logo.png`  
**Recommended export:** transparent PNG, 2000 × 2000 px, sRGB, with generous clear space. Also keep a high-quality source export if Nano Banana Pro produces one.

## Nano Banana Pro prompt
Create a premium, original, timeless luxury men's grooming studio logo for the fictional brand “ATELIER 09”. The brand descriptor is “GROOMING CULTURE”. Design a confident, precise, editorial identity inspired by contemporary European barbershop craft, architectural geometry, bespoke tailoring, and high-end fragrance packaging. Create a distinctive custom monogram combining A and 09, with excellent optical balance, clean vector-like edges, controlled negative space, and a sophisticated wordmark. Palette: warm ivory and muted antique brass, with an optional tiny acid-lime accent (#D3F36B). Make the mark feel authentic, restrained, masculine, and premium—not generic, not vintage clip-art, not a barber pole logo. Center the complete logo, keep all lettering perfectly spelled exactly as “ATELIER 09” and “GROOMING CULTURE”, ensure no cropped edges, no mockup, no wall/signage, no product scene, no extra words, no watermark. Transparent background, square composition, logo artwork only, crisp high-resolution finish.

## Negative directions
No barber pole, scissors crossed over a moustache, skulls, crowns, random glyphs, misspelled text, fake 3D embossing, glowing effects, noisy textures, photographic background, mockup, border frame, or extra slogans.

## Acceptance checklist
- Entire logo is visible and centered; nothing is cropped.
- “ATELIER 09” and “GROOMING CULTURE” are spelled correctly.
- Transparent background, not white/black baked into the image.
- Strong contrast at both 48 px and 500 px.
- Keep the final filename exactly `atelier-09-logo.png`.
