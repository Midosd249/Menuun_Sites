# Service photography

## Expected filenames
- `signature-cut.webp`
- `beard-sculpt.webp`
- `full-ritual.webp`
- `classic-shave.webp`
- `skin-reset.webp`
- `occasion-ready.webp`

Recommended size: 1200 × 1500 px, portrait 4:5, WebP, high-quality export. Keep lighting, color grade, and visual realism consistent across the set.

## Prompt template (generate one image per service)
Create one photorealistic luxury men's grooming editorial photograph for the ATELIER 09 service “[SERVICE NAME]”. Show the specific service clearly: [DESCRIBE THE ACTION OR DETAIL]. Contemporary Riyadh grooming atelier, dark charcoal and warm walnut interior, premium stainless-steel tools, soft directional tungsten lighting, deep olive-black shadows, subtle muted lime accent, natural skin texture, refined masculine editorial art direction, shallow depth of field, authentic professional barber technique. Vertical 4:5 composition, subject placed centrally with enough breathing room for a responsive crop. No text, no logo, no watermark, no collage, no visible brand labels.

Replace [SERVICE NAME] and [DESCRIBE THE ACTION OR DETAIL] with:
- Signature Cut: barber detailing a clean modern haircut with clippers and comb.
- Beard Sculpt: precise beard line-up with a professional trimmer.
- Full Ritual: warm towel and careful grooming treatment in a calm studio.
- Classic Shave: traditional close shave with a straight razor handled safely by a professional.
- Skin Reset: refined facial skincare treatment for a male client.
- Occasion Ready: final styling and finishing touches before an event.

Do not reuse the same generated image for every service; each image should visually communicate its specific service.
