# Business vertical strategy

## Shared platform, distinct experiences
Reuse the editing, localization, asset, publishing, SEO, domain, access-control, and analytics platform. Do not force every industry into one generic template. Each vertical gets its own information architecture, section schema, visual direction, and conversion goals.

## Initial vertical: salons and beauty
- Hero and primary booking/contact CTA
- Services, prices, durations
- Team and credentials
- Gallery and before/after (with consent)
- Location, hours, contact and social links
- Offers and FAQs

## Future vertical: clinics and wellness
- Specialties and services
- Clinician profiles and qualifications
- Appointment request and location
- Insurance/payment information only when verified by the business
- Privacy-conscious contact forms; no collection of sensitive medical information unless a reviewed compliance design explicitly permits it
- Trust, credentials, and accessibility-first content

## Future vertical: restaurants and cafés
- Menu categories and items
- Opening hours and location
- Reservations/order links
- Dietary/allergen content only when verified
- Gallery and events

## Future vertical: professional services
- Expertise, case studies, team, testimonials with permission, contact and lead forms

## Content safety and integrity
Business-supplied claims (prices, medical qualifications, allergens, opening hours) must remain explicitly editable and must not be invented by AI. AI can draft; the customer must verify before publishing.
