## Immediate implications
1. Do not bolt a live backend onto the existing static demo before defining the schema, auth and access policies.
2. The current template is visually useful but not an app foundation by itself. Introduce build/test structure incrementally, preserve the page, and split responsibilities when the first dashboard/API needs them.
3. Prioritize local ownership and stable serving of the video/poster/images after confirming asset ownership/licensing. Until then, retain the proven source and test it after every deployment.
4. Appointment creation must be atomic and server-authoritative; UI-only checks cannot prevent double-booking.
5. RLS and grants must be designed together for each exposed table. Include positive and negative tests for anon, owner, non-member and a second salon.
6. A deployment marked READY and HTTP 200 do not prove the full user journey. Add browser tests for video element state, booking, QR destination, owner inbox, and Arabic/English responsive behavior.
